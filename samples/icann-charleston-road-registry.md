# ICANN top-level domain applications for Charleston Road Registry

Charleston Road Registry is Alphabet's DNS registry. In 2012, it applied to ICANN for new top-level domains, with an application for each domain covering networking, character sets, policy, and business purpose. Working with a cross-functional Google Engineering team, I drafted and edited these applications, and wrote custom scripts to generate over a hundred applications from a single-source system.
