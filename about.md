# About

I'm a technical documentation leader with more than 20 years of experience driving measurable impact at Google and Meta. I build scalable content systems and lead high-performing teams that improve developer productivity and customer success.

At Meta, I spent seven years as a documentation engineer and then an information solutions manager. I led security documentation for an organization of more than 3,000 security engineers, created a writing guide adopted by thousands of Meta engineers, and later managed a 20-person internal developer support team. Before Meta, I spent ten years as a technical writer at Google, writing integration and administrator guides for Postini and Google Enterprise services. Most recently, I refreshed the admin documentation for Serval, an AI-based IT platform, as docs-as-code with Mintlify and Claude Code.

I have a BA in Cognitive Science from UC Berkeley and an MBA from the University of Phoenix. I'm based in the San Francisco Bay Area and active in the Write the Docs community.
